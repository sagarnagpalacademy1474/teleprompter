Prompter, voice tracking prototype

Phase 1 test build . Script and settings are saved on this device only.
How to test: open the prompter and tap Mic on once (the browser asks for microphone permission). Then say the wake phrase, or just start reading the first line. Try these on purpose: add a few lines of your own, change some words, repeat a line two or three times, skip a sentence, go back to an earlier line. The text should wait while you go off script and catch up when you come back. When you finish the last line and stop for 2 seconds, the take ends and the script returns to the top, ready for the next take. Say the restart phrase after a short pause to begin a fresh take at any time. Tap any word to jump there. Keyboard: Space start or pause, R restart, Esc back.

Hinglish scripts: choose "Hinglish / Hindi (Roman script)" as the speech language. The mic then listens in Hindi, and the app converts what it hears to Roman letters before matching, so a script typed as "gaadi" still matches when the mic writes गाड़ी.
